WHY THESE LOCATIONS

From frontage to platform

01 / STATION FRONTAGE

Façade in an RCC bay

The façade drawing labels existing RCC columns and slab. The curved frame is shown within the station building's arched frontage, approached from the forecourt.

02 / PASSENGER ROUTE

Escalator at concourse access

The steel support stands beside the island platform's upward route to a covered concourse connection. The escalator and bridge around it show context; their dimensions are illustrative.

03 / PLATFORM

12 m canopy over the island

The C.O.P. section spans the platform, separate from the façade and escalator frame. Track and overhead equipment continue through the station scene.

Station form draws on a photograph of Kota Junction’s clock-fronted building ↗ and the redevelopment rendering and project description ↗. Locations within the station are interpretive because the three supplied structural sheets do not provide a site plan.
